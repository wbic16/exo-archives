Terse: Multi-Dimensional Text

Traditional text files are great. You can store an entire page of text with zero overhead. A typical page is 2-4 KB in size (80 x 50 = 4,000). There's no extra formatting or markup.

Text is the OG WYSIWYG format.

1/27
---

Mon Dec 12 05:53:54 +0000 2022
Let's review what text files are from first-principles:

* Text = a serialized stream of characters (1D)
* Page = an array of lines (2D)

What might text in higher dimensions look like?
How would we make sense of it?

These are the questions that lead me to develop Terse.

2/27

Mon Dec 12 06:05:04 +0000 2022
The AGI Era

Terse Text is designed for 2040 and beyond. It is assumed that humanity will be using systems that routinely store petabytes of data and that we will have brain interfaces which allow us to interact with our computers in simulated hyperspace.

This corresponds to the first 4 dimensions of Terse (Columns, Lines, Scrolls, and Sections).

  100^11 = 10 million petabytes

Terse Text (.t) is a shell for existing text formats. The format is intentionally vague - leaving users the freedom to tinker.

I'm proposing that historic ASCII control codes can be retired and re-purposed for this improvement.
