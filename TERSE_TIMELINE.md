## Terse → Phext Evolution

### What Terse Was
- **GUI text editor** (Windows, C#/WPF)
- **Tree-based structure** (hierarchical nodes)
- **"Additional dimensions"** (undefined, but clearly > 2D)
- **VIM integration** (keyboard-first navigation)
- **Dark mode, scrollbars, real-time updates** (polished UX)

### What Phext Became
- **Plain text format** (no GUI required, just delimiters)
- **11-dimensional structure** (library.shelf.series / collection.volume.book / chapter.section.scroll)
- **Coordinate navigation** (1.1.1/1.1.1/1.1.1 = precise addressing)
- **Database backend (SQ)** (not just editor, but storage + API)
- **Multi-tool ecosystem** (phext-shell, phext-notepad, libphext-rs, libphext-node, SQ)

**The shift:** From "editor with extra dimensions" to "plain text extended to 11 dimensions as a coordination substrate."
