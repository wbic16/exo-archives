## Why "Terse" → "Phext"?

### Terse Etymology
- **Terse** (adjective): Brief and to the point; using few words
- Emphasized the compactness of the format
- "Terse Text" = compact multi-dimensional text

### Phext Etymology
- **Phext** = "Plain text, extended"
- More descriptive of what it actually is
- Better branding (unique, searchable, pronounceable)
- "Phext" sounds futuristic, "Terse" sounds limiting

**The rebrand was about clarity:** "Extended plain text" explains the concept better than "brief text."
